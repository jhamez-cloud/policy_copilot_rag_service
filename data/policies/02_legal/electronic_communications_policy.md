## 1. Electronic notices

The operator may communicate with users through the registered email address, in-product notifications, account dashboards or [OFFICIAL_NOTICE_CHANNEL]. Users should keep contact details accurate and check service notices.

## 2. Electronic records

The platform may create electronic records of account actions, consent choices, transactions, uploads, support requests and policy acknowledgements. Such records may be retained as described in the Privacy and Retention Policies and may be used for legitimate operational, evidential and legal purposes.

## 3. Consent and acknowledgements

Where the law requires consent, the operator will use an appropriate affirmative action and retain a record of the choice, its scope and the applicable notice version. A general statement that a user visited a website is not treated as consent where affirmative consent is legally required.

## 4. Updating contact information

Users are responsible for maintaining a valid email address and reviewing important notices. The operator will use additional notice methods where required by law or where email is not reasonably sufficient.

## 5. Legal effect

Electronic communications and records may have legal effect subject to applicable law, including Ghana's Electronic Transactions Act, 2008 (Act 772), as amended. This policy does not replace any formal signing requirement imposed by law.
