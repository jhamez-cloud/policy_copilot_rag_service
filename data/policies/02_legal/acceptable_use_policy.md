## 1. Purpose

This policy protects users, document owners, the operator and third parties. It applies to accounts, uploads, queries, generated outputs, APIs and all other use of [PRODUCT_NAME].

## 2. Users must not

Users must not:
- Upload content they do not have the right or lawful authority to use.
- Violate copyright, confidentiality, privacy, trade secret or other third-party rights.
- Use the platform to harass, threaten, defraud, impersonate or unlawfully discriminate against others.
- Upload malicious files, malware, exploit code intended to compromise systems, or content designed to attack the service.
- Attempt to bypass authentication, quotas, access controls, tenant separation or technical restrictions.
- Probe, scan or test systems without written authorisation.
- Reverse engineer or interfere with the service except to the extent such restriction is prohibited by law.
- Use the service for unlawful surveillance or unauthorised profiling.
- Submit personal or sensitive information without an appropriate lawful basis and necessary permissions.
- Misrepresent AI-generated answers as verified professional advice or as quotations from a source that does not support them.
- Use the service in a way that violates applicable law or another person's rights.

## 3. Sensitive and confidential information

Users should upload only information necessary for their intended task. Do not upload highly sensitive personal data, authentication secrets, payment-card data, confidential business information or legally privileged material unless the user has assessed the risks, has authority to do so and the service has been expressly approved for that data category.

## 4. Enforcement

The operator may reject files, remove content, limit processing, suspend access or terminate accounts where reasonably necessary to enforce this policy, protect the service or comply with law. Enforcement will be proportionate where practicable and subject to mandatory legal rights.

## 5. Reporting

Report suspected abuse or policy violations to [ABUSE_REPORT_EMAIL], including relevant account, document or request identifiers. Do not send unnecessary copies of sensitive content in a report.
