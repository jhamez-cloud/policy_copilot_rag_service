## 1. Informational assistance only

AI-generated answers are provided for general informational and document-navigation purposes. They are not legal, medical, financial, tax, academic, safety or other professional advice.

## 2. Accuracy limitations

AI systems can misunderstand questions, overlook relevant passages, misinterpret tables, confuse similar terms, produce outdated information or generate statements that are not supported by the source documents.

## 3. Source limitations

The assistant can only use information made available to its retrieval process and any other context explicitly configured by the product. If a relevant document was not uploaded, was not indexed, was deleted, could not be parsed or was not retrieved, the answer may omit it.

## 4. Citations

Where a response provides citations, users should open the cited document and check the relevant passage. Citations may be incomplete or may not support every part of an answer. A missing citation does not establish that a statement is false, and a citation does not establish that it is true.

## 5. Recommended verification

For important decisions:
1. Read the original document.
2. Check the quoted or cited passage in context.
3. Compare conflicting documents and their dates.
4. Confirm that the document is current and authoritative.
5. Consult a qualified professional where appropriate.

## 6. No guarantee of completeness

The operator does not warrant that the assistant will find every relevant passage or answer every question. The user remains responsible for assessing the suitability of an output before relying on it.

## 7. Feedback

Users may report an incorrect or unsupported answer at [AI_FEEDBACK_CHANNEL]. Include the question, answer reference and relevant source identifier where possible, but avoid sending additional sensitive content.
