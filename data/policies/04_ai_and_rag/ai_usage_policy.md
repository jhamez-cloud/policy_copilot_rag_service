## 1. Purpose

This policy governs AI-assisted features in [PRODUCT_NAME], including document-based question answering, retrieval, summarisation and generated responses.

## 2. How AI is used

The service uses a RAG workflow: it retrieves relevant content from the user's authorised document collection and supplies that content as context to a generative AI model. Unless a separate feature explicitly states otherwise, uploading a document does not fine-tune or retrain the underlying model.

## 3. User responsibilities

Users must:
- Upload only documents they are authorised to use.
- Avoid uploading unnecessary personal, confidential or sensitive information.
- Ask questions for lawful purposes.
- Check generated answers against the original source.
- Avoid relying solely on AI output for high-impact decisions.
- Report unsafe, misleading or improperly sourced answers through [FEEDBACK_CHANNEL].

## 4. Prohibited AI use

Users must not use the AI features to facilitate unlawful activity, infringe rights, impersonate others, conduct unauthorised surveillance, bypass security controls or generate content that violates the Acceptable Use Policy.

## 5. Human oversight

The platform is an assistance tool. Users and organisations remain responsible for human review where decisions affect legal rights, employment, education, access to essential services, health, finance, safety or other significant interests.

## 6. Provider processing

AI requests may be processed by third-party model providers identified in [SUBPROCESSOR_LIST_URL]. The operator must disclose what content is sent, where it is processed, whether it is retained and whether it may be used for model improvement.

## 7. No guaranteed model behaviour

AI models can produce incorrect, incomplete, biased or fabricated statements. The presence of a citation does not prove that the cited passage supports every sentence. Users should inspect the cited material.
