## 1. What is [PRODUCT_NAME]?

[PRODUCT_NAME] is an AI-assisted document question-answering platform. It allows an authorised user to upload supported documents, have their contents processed for retrieval, and ask questions that are answered using relevant information from those documents.

The platform is designed to help users find, understand and navigate information contained in documents they choose to provide.

## 2. User-controlled knowledge

The user is responsible for selecting and uploading the documents that form their knowledge source. The platform does not independently know which private documents a user wants to use and does not require the user to upload any particular document.

Answers may be limited when the uploaded collection does not contain relevant information, when a file cannot be processed, or when the retrieved content is incomplete or ambiguous.

## 3. RAG, not automatic fine-tuning

Unless a separately described feature expressly says otherwise, [PRODUCT_NAME] uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). The service retrieves relevant passages from indexed documents and supplies them as context to an AI model. This is different from training or fine-tuning the model.
