## 1. Ownership

The policy library is maintained by [POLICY_OWNER_OR_TEAM]. Each document must have an identifier, version, effective date, review date and status.

## 2. Review triggers

Policies should be reviewed:
- At least [REVIEW_INTERVAL].
- When a new feature changes data collection or processing.
- When a provider, hosting region or subprocessor changes.
- When file types, limits, pricing or retention rules change.
- After a security incident or material complaint.
- When relevant laws, regulations or regulator guidance change.

## 3. Version control

Use semantic or sequential document versions consistently. Keep prior versions in an internal archive with their effective dates. Do not allow superseded documents to remain active in the public RAG index unless clearly labelled as historical.

## 4. Publication

Only approved documents with `status: published` should be exposed to users. Material changes should be communicated through [NOTICE_CHANNEL] and users should be asked for renewed consent where legally required.

## 5. RAG indexing

When a document is updated:
1. Assign the new version and effective date.
2. Remove or mark the previous version as superseded.
3. Re-index the approved document.
4. Verify that retrieval returns the current version.
5. Preserve historical versions separately if needed for audit or contractual evidence.

## 6. Change log

Maintain an internal change log recording the document ID, old and new versions, summary of changes, reviewer, approval date and publication date.
