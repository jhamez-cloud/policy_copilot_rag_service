## 1. What does [PRODUCT_NAME] do?

[PRODUCT_NAME] lets you upload supported documents and ask questions. Its RAG service retrieves relevant information from your indexed documents and uses it as context for an AI-generated answer.

## 2. Does the platform ask me to upload documents?

No. You decide which documents to upload. The service can only use documents that are available to its retrieval process and that you are authorised to provide.

## 3. Does uploading a file fine-tune the AI model?

Not by default. RAG retrieves relevant passages and supplies them to a model as context. It is not the same as fine-tuning, which changes model parameters through additional training.

## 4. Which file types can I upload?

The currently supported format is Markdown (`.md`). Check [UPLOAD_LIMITS_URL] for the current rules and any newly supported formats.

## 5. What is the maximum file size?

The current maximum is [MAX_FILE_SIZE]. Limits may differ by plan or change over time. Check the upload screen or [UPLOAD_LIMITS_URL] for the live value.

## 6. Why did my file fail to process?

Possible reasons include an unsupported format, file-size limit, invalid encoding, corrupt content, processing failure or a temporary provider outage. Check the file status and error message.

## 7. Does the AI read every document for every question?

The RAG system searches the indexed collection and retrieves passages considered relevant to the question. It may not retrieve every relevant passage.

## 8. Are answers guaranteed to be correct?

No. AI responses may be inaccurate or incomplete. Open the cited source and verify important information independently.

## 9. Can I use the answers as legal or medical advice?

No. The service is an informational tool, not a substitute for a qualified professional.

## 10. Who is responsible for uploaded documents?

You are responsible for ensuring that you own the documents or have the permissions and lawful authority required to upload and process them.

## 11. Does the platform own my documents?

No. You retain rights you hold in your content. You grant the operator only the limited licence needed to provide and maintain the service, as described in the User Content and Ownership Policy.

## 12. Are my documents used to train AI models?

The platform's RAG workflow does not inherently fine-tune a model. Third-party provider processing and retention must be described in the Privacy Policy and subprocessor list. See [MODEL_PROVIDER_DATA_POLICY_URL].

## 13. Can other users see my files?

The platform is intended to restrict access to authorised users and workspaces. Report any suspected unauthorised access immediately to [SECURITY_EMAIL]. The operator must ensure that its deployed access controls match this statement.

## 14. Can I delete my documents?

Use [DELETION_METHOD]. Deletion may take time to propagate to derived indexes, provider systems and backups, as described in the Data Retention and Deletion Policy.

## 15. Will the service always be free?

Not necessarily. Pricing, free access and quotas are described in the Subscription and Pricing Policy. The operator may introduce paid plans or change limits with appropriate notice and subject to applicable law.

## 16. Can file limits or supported formats change?

Yes. Limits and formats may change due to technical capacity, security, provider restrictions or plan changes. Current limits are shown at [UPLOAD_LIMITS_URL].

## 17. Does GDPR apply?

GDPR applies where its legal scope requirements are met. A user's location, the operator's establishment and the nature of the service and processing can matter. See the GDPR Compliance Policy.
