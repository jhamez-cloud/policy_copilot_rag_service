## 1. Support channels

Users may contact [SUPPORT_EMAIL], use [IN-APP_SUPPORT_METHOD] or visit [HELP_CENTRE_URL].

## 2. Information to provide

A support request should include:
- Account email or user identifier.
- A brief description of the issue.
- Approximate date and time.
- File or request identifier, if relevant.
- Relevant error message.

Do not send passwords, access tokens, payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive document content.

## 3. Response targets

The operator aims to acknowledge ordinary support requests within [SUPPORT_ACKNOWLEDGEMENT_TARGET] and respond according to severity and available resources. These targets are not guarantees unless stated in a paid service agreement.

## 4. Priority

Security incidents, suspected cross-account data exposure and privacy rights requests should be clearly labelled and routed to [SECURITY_OR_PRIVACY_EMAIL].

## 5. Complaints

Users may submit a formal complaint to [COMPLAINTS_EMAIL]. The operator will review it fairly, retain an appropriate record and communicate the outcome or next steps.

## 6. External rights

This internal support process does not prevent users from contacting a competent regulator, supervisory authority or court where they have a legal right to do so.
