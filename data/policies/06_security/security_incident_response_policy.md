## 1. Purpose

This policy describes the operator's intended process for identifying, containing, investigating and responding to security incidents affecting [PRODUCT_NAME].

## 2. What is an incident?

An incident may include unauthorised access, data disclosure, account compromise, malicious file activity, service disruption, data corruption, loss of availability or a suspected personal-data breach.

## 3. Response stages

1. **Detection:** Receive alerts, reports or monitoring signals.
2. **Triage:** Assess credibility, severity, systems and data potentially affected.
3. **Containment:** Limit access, revoke credentials, isolate affected services or suspend processing where appropriate.
4. **Investigation:** Preserve relevant logs and determine the scope, cause and likely impact.
5. **Remediation:** Remove the cause, restore secure operation and address vulnerabilities.
6. **Notification:** Notify customers, affected individuals and regulators where required by applicable law or contract.
7. **Recovery:** Restore services and verify integrity.
8. **Review:** Document lessons learned and corrective actions.

## 4. Personal-data breaches

The operator will assess whether an incident constitutes a personal-data breach and identify the relevant controller/processor role. Where GDPR applies, a controller must notify the competent authority within 72 hours of awareness where Article 33 requires notification. A processor must notify its controller without undue delay. Ghanaian statutory and regulatory requirements must also be assessed.

## 5. Customer communication

Business customers should report suspected incidents to [SECURITY_EMAIL]. The operator will provide information reasonably available to support the customer's assessment and legal obligations.

## 6. User reporting

Users should report suspected security incidents promptly and include relevant timestamps, account identifiers and error references. Do not send passwords, access tokens or unnecessary copies of personal data.
