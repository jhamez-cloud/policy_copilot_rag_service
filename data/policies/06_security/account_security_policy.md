## 1. User responsibilities

Users should:
- Use a unique, strong password where password authentication is available.
- Enable multi-factor authentication where offered.
- Protect email accounts and devices used to access the service.
- Avoid sharing login credentials.
- Sign out of shared or public devices.
- Keep recovery details current.
- Report suspected compromise promptly to [SECURITY_EMAIL].

## 2. Authentication

The platform may use [AUTHENTICATION_METHODS]. The operator should apply secure password storage, session management, rate limiting and account recovery controls appropriate to the authentication method.

## 3. Suspicious activity

The operator may require re-authentication, revoke sessions or temporarily restrict access where necessary to investigate suspected compromise or protect an account.

## 4. Account sharing

Account sharing is governed by the applicable plan and workspace features. Users must not share credentials in a way that bypasses user-level permissions, billing limits or audit controls.

## 5. Recovery

Account recovery requests must follow [ACCOUNT_RECOVERY_PROCESS]. Support staff should never ask users to disclose passwords, one-time codes or private authentication keys.
