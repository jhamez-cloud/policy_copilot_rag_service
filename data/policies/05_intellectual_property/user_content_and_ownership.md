## 1. User ownership

As between the user and the operator, the user retains ownership of the documents and other content they upload, to the extent the user owns those rights. Uploading does not transfer ownership to [PRODUCT_NAME].

## 2. Limited service licence

The user grants the operator a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence for the duration necessary to provide the service to host, store, reproduce, extract, parse, transform, chunk, embed, index, retrieve, transmit and display the content as instructed or authorised by the user.

This licence is limited to providing, maintaining, securing and supporting the service, complying with law and handling support or disputes. It does not grant the operator a general right to sell user documents or publicly publish them.

## 3. Third-party rights

A user may upload another party's material only where the user has permission or another lawful basis. The user must comply with confidentiality obligations and restrictions imposed by the document owner.

## 4. Model improvement and training

Unless the product clearly discloses a separate feature and obtains any required permission or legal basis, user documents, prompts and outputs will not be used by the operator to train or fine-tune its own general-purpose models.

## 5. Deletion

When a user deletes content or closes an account, the operator will handle the content according to the Data Retention and Deletion Policy. Some limited copies may remain temporarily in backups or be retained where lawfully required.

## 6. Feedback

If users voluntarily submit suggestions or product feedback, the operator may use that feedback to improve the service without transferring ownership of the user's underlying documents. Do not include confidential information in general feedback unless requested through an appropriate protected channel.
