## 1. Reporting channel

A rights holder or authorised representative may report alleged copyright infringement to [COPYRIGHT_CONTACT_EMAIL] or [COPYRIGHT_REPORT_URL].

## 2. Information to include

A report should contain:
- The complainant's name and contact details.
- Identification of the copyrighted work.
- Identification of the allegedly infringing material and its location in the service.
- A clear explanation of the rights claimed and the alleged infringement.
- Evidence of authority to act for the rights holder, where applicable.
- A statement that the information is accurate and that the complainant is authorised to submit the report.

Do not require a person to make a statement under penalty of perjury unless the applicable legal process requires it.

## 3. Review

The operator will review reports in good faith, request clarification where necessary and take proportionate action where appropriate. Possible actions include restricting access to the identified content, disabling a relevant account feature, preserving evidence or rejecting an unsupported report.

## 4. Notice to affected user

Where lawful and appropriate, the operator may notify the uploader and provide an opportunity to respond. Notice may be delayed or withheld where required to protect an investigation, comply with law or prevent harm.

## 5. Counter-notice or dispute

An affected user may submit a response to [COPYRIGHT_CONTACT_EMAIL], explaining why the content is authorised or why the report is mistaken. The operator may request supporting documentation.

## 6. Repeat infringement

Where appropriate and lawful, repeated or serious infringement may result in account restrictions or termination.

## 7. No automatic legal conclusion

The operator's handling of a report is not a court determination of ownership or infringement. The parties retain any rights to seek remedies through competent authorities or courts.
