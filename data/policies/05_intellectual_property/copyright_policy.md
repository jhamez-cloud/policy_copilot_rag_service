## 1. General principle

Copyright and other intellectual property rights belong to their respective owners. The platform does not give users ownership of documents uploaded by other people or organisations.

## 2. User responsibility

Before uploading a document, the user must confirm that they:
- Own the relevant rights; or
- Have a licence, permission, legal exception or other lawful basis allowing the upload and processing; and
- Are permitted to use the content for the intended purpose.

Access to a file does not automatically grant permission to copy, upload, index, distribute or commercially use it.

## 3. Copyright-protected material

Protected content may include books, articles, reports, photographs, illustrations, music, films, databases, software and other eligible works. Copyright exceptions and limitations vary by jurisdiction and context. Users are responsible for obtaining advice where the legal position is uncertain.

## 4. Platform intellectual property

The platform's software, source code, design, branding, logos, documentation and proprietary materials are owned by or licensed to the operator, except for third-party components and materials identified under their respective licences.

## 5. AI output

Ownership and permitted use of AI-generated output may depend on applicable law, the input material, third-party rights and model-provider terms. The operator does not warrant that every output is unique, copyrightable or free of third-party rights. Users must review outputs before publication or commercial use.

## 6. Open-source software

Third-party and open-source components remain subject to their own licence terms. Nothing in this policy removes obligations imposed by those licences.
