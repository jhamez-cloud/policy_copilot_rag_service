## 3. Controller obligations

The Controller is responsible for:
- Establishing a lawful basis and providing required privacy notices.
- Ensuring it has authority to provide the data and issue processing instructions.
- Minimising data and avoiding unnecessary sensitive data.
- Responding to data subject requests, with reasonable assistance from the Processor.
- Ensuring its use of the service complies with applicable law.

## 4. Processor obligations

The Processor will:
- Process personal data only on documented instructions, including transfers, unless law requires otherwise.
- Ensure persons authorised to process data are bound by confidentiality.
- Implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures.
- Respect the conditions for appointing subprocessors.
- Assist the Controller, taking into account the nature of processing, with data subject requests and compliance duties.
- Notify the Controller of a personal-data breach without undue delay after becoming aware.
- At the Controller's choice, delete or return personal data after the end of services, unless law requires retention.
- Make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance and support audits under agreed reasonable conditions.
