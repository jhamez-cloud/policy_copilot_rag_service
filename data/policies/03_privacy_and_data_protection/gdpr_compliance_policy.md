## 1. Applicability

The EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) applies only where its material and territorial scope requirements are met. A Ghana-based operator may be within scope when processing occurs in the context of an EU establishment or when it offers goods or services to people in the EU or monitors their behaviour there, as described in Article 3.

This document explains the safeguards the operator intends to apply where GDPR-covered processing occurs. It is not a claim that every GDPR obligation applies to every user or every processing activity.

## 2. Roles

The operator may act as:
- **Controller:** when it determines the purposes and means of processing account, billing, security and platform-operation data.
- **Processor:** when it processes customer-controlled document content on behalf of a business customer under that customer's instructions.

The role must be assessed for each processing activity. A customer acting as controller is responsible for its own lawful basis, notices and instructions.

## 3. Principles

For GDPR-covered processing, the operator will work toward the principles in Article 5:
- Lawfulness, fairness and transparency.
- Purpose limitation.
- Data minimisation.
- Accuracy.
- Storage limitation.
- Integrity and confidentiality.
- Accountability.

## 4. Data subject rights

Subject to applicable conditions and exceptions, individuals may request:
- Access to their personal data.
- Rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- Erasure.
- Restriction of processing.
- Data portability.
- Objection to certain processing.
- Withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent.
- Information about automated decision-making where Article 22 applies.

Requests should be sent to [PRIVACY_EMAIL] with enough information to locate the relevant data. The operator may take proportionate steps to verify identity and will respond within the period required by applicable law. GDPR generally provides a one-month response period, subject to lawful extensions and notification requirements.

## 5. Legal bases and consent

Each processing purpose must have a documented legal basis under Article 6 where GDPR applies. Special-category data requires an additional applicable condition under Article 9. Consent must be specific, informed, freely given, unambiguous and withdrawable where relied upon.

## 6. Processors and contracts

Where the operator acts as a processor, it will use a written contract meeting applicable Article 28 requirements, including documented instructions, confidentiality, security, subprocessors, assistance with rights requests and incidents, deletion or return, and audit information.

## 7. Security and breach handling

The operator will implement risk-appropriate safeguards and assess personal-data breaches. Where GDPR applies, the controller must notify the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of a personal-data breach where Article 33 requires notification. A processor must notify its controller without undue delay. Affected individuals must be notified where Article 34 requires it.

## 8. International transfers

Transfers outside the European Economic Area must use a valid GDPR Chapter V transfer mechanism where required. The operator will assess the destination, transfer mechanism and supplementary safeguards as appropriate.

## 9. Supervisory authority and complaints

Individuals may lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority, particularly in the EU Member State of their habitual residence, place of work or alleged infringement, where GDPR provides that right.

## 10. Requests and contact

Submit requests to [PRIVACY_EMAIL]. Include the account email, request type and relevant workspace or document identifiers. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

## 11. Accountability records

The operator should maintain records of processing activities where required, data protection impact assessments where required, processor contracts, transfer assessments, security assessments, consent records and documented retention schedules.

## 12. Important qualification

This policy must be checked against the real service architecture and current law. It does not itself appoint an EU representative, DPO or establish a lawful international transfer mechanism. Those steps must be completed if legally required.
