## 2. Information we may process

Depending on the features used, the platform may process:
- Account information: name, email, organisation and authentication identifiers.
- Service records: account settings, plan, quota and billing status.
- Uploaded content: original files and document metadata.
- Derived content: extracted text, chunks, embeddings, indexes and retrieval metadata.
- Interaction data: questions, prompts, generated answers, citations and conversation history.
- Technical data: IP address, device/browser information, timestamps, diagnostic events and security logs.
- Support information: messages and attachments sent to support.
- Payment information: payment status and transaction references. Payment-card details should be handled by a compliant payment provider where possible and should not be stored by the platform unless expressly designed and assessed for that purpose.

The operator must remove categories that are not actually collected and add any omitted categories before publication.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases

We process information to:
- Create and administer accounts.
- Authenticate users and enforce access permissions.
- Receive, validate, extract, index and retrieve uploaded documents.
- Generate answers to user questions.
- Provide customer support and communicate service notices.
- Maintain service reliability, prevent abuse and investigate security events.
- Process payments and meet accounting obligations.
- Comply with legal duties and establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
- Improve the service where a valid legal basis exists and the use is consistent with the notices given.

For GDPR-covered processing, the applicable legal basis may include contract necessity, legal obligation, legitimate interests or consent, depending on the specific purpose. Consent must not be used as a blanket substitute for identifying the correct legal basis.

For processing governed by Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), the operator will identify and document the applicable lawful basis and comply with the Act's principles and requirements.
